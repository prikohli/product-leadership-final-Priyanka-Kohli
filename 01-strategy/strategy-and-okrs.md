# Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** For a superintendent, Meridian is the fastest way to document work, communicate issues, and close the day without extra paperwork.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Large North American commercial contractors; mobile workflows for photo documentation, issue reporting, daily logs, and safety observations.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Turn photos, voice notes, and text messages into structured project records automatically, eliminating duplicate data entry for field teams.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** World-class mobile capture, AI extraction and tagging, offline reliability, and deep integration with enterprise project systems.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Measure weekly active field users, percentage of records created through camera/voice, and hours of documentation time saved; review adoption and workflow friction every quarter.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will not build a desktop-first project management experience for field users.
Why: Many customers would ask for richer forms, more dashboards, more workflows, and more configuration in the field. Those features are valuable, but pursuing them would push superintendents and foremen back into the behavior they already avoid: sitting at a laptop entering data.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Make Meridian the daily operating system for field teams by eliminating manual project documentation.
- **KR1:** Increase weekly active field users (superintendents and foremen) from 25% to 60% of licensed field workers.
- **KR2:** Increase the percentage of project records created through photo, voice, or text capture from 10% to 70%.
- **KR3:** Reduce average time spent on daily documentation by field personnel from 60 minutes per day to 20 minutes per day.

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** Do superintendents avoid Meridian because it takes too much time, or because they don't see enough value in using it? AI is pointing that I must have misdiagnosed the customer problem.
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** I'd change the hard no to "no desktop data entry" and strengthen the evidence that documentation friction is the root problem, while defending the focus on field users, a narrow set of workflows, and the goal of eliminating manual documentation.
