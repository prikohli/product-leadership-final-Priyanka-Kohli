## Refine and complete your charter
- **What We Own. What this team owns.:** Own field adoption outcomes, mobile workflows, and the platform foundations that support them.
- **What is out of scope.:** Enterprise-only features, executive reporting, and workforce management that do not improve field adoption.
- **Cross-boundary decisions that need a joint call.:** Engineering resource shifts, shared platform changes, and roadmap changes that affect team OKRs.
- **How We Decide. Who decides feature and scope calls.:** PM 1 decides field experience and adoption priorities; PM 2 decides enterprise features; shared platform decisions are made jointly.
- **How cross-team conflicts escalate.:** If PMs cannot agree within 48 hours, the issue is escalated with documented trade-offs and OKR impact.
- **Who resolves escalations from outside the team.:** The Product Director (or product leader over both teams) makes the final decision based on strategy, OKRs, and business impact.
